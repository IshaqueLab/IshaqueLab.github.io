# Privacy

This site is a static website hosted by GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics.

## Third-party services

| Service | What it does | Data sent | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) | Hosts the site | Your IP address and request headers, as for any website; see [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) | All pages |
| Google Fonts (Google LLC) | Delivers the IBM Plex Sans typeface | Your IP address and browser details, to Google | All pages |
| OpenAlex (OurResearch) | Publication list | The lab's ORCID and your IP address | Home, Publications |
| BIP! (Athena Research Center) | Citation-based indicators for selected papers | The paper DOI and your IP address | Software, Publications |
| Altmetric (Digital Science) | Attention badges for selected papers | The paper DOI, your IP address; Altmetric may set cookies | Publications |
| Dimensions (Digital Science) | Citation badges for selected papers | The paper DOI, your IP address; Dimensions may set cookies | Publications |

You can block any of these with a content blocker; the pages remain readable without them.

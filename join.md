# Join the lab

## Internships and theses

We welcome Bachelor's and Master's students for internships and thesis projects in bioinformatics, computational biology, molecular biosciences and 'omics. Projects typically run for eight weeks or longer and are co-supervised day to day by a doctoral researcher or postdoc in the team.

Email <a href="mailto:{{ site.email }}">Naveed Ishaque</a> with a short description of your background, your availability and which of our <a href="{{ '/research/' | relative_url }}">research areas</a> interests you.

## Doctoral and postdoctoral positions

Open positions are posted here and on the <a href="https://www.bihealth.org">BIH careers pages</a>. Speculative enquiries from strong candidates with a background in computational biology, machine learning or statistics are welcome at any time.
